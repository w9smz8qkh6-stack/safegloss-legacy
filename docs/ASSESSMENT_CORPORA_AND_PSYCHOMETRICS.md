# Assessment Corpora & Psychometric Foundations

---

## Purpose

This document defines how Safegloss models
**standards-aligned assessment practices**
without copying copyrighted tests.

---

## Reference Assessment Corpora

We model the *structure and style* of items from:

- NAEP Reading Framework
- Cambridge Primary English
- IB PYP / MYP assessments
- Released state reading assessments (public items)

We do NOT store or reproduce test content.

---

## What Is Extracted from Corpora

- Question stem patterns
- Typical Bloom distributions
- Distractor design strategies
- Language register (neutral, formal)
- Item pacing within a quiz

---

## Psychometric Principles Applied

### Construct Validity
- Items measure reading comprehension only
- No construct-irrelevant difficulty

### Reliability
- Similar items behave similarly
- Consistent difficulty per Bloom level

### Item Independence
- No question depends on another
- Each item stands alone

---

## Difficulty Calibration (Heuristic)

Difficulty is controlled by:
- Bloom level
- Question type
- Linguistic complexity
- Inference demand

NOT by:
- trick questions
- confusing wording
- rare vocabulary

---

## Fairness & Accessibility

- Avoid idioms
- Avoid culturally specific references
- Prefer literal language for younger ages
- Allow accommodations via Study Mode

---

## Why This Matters

These principles ensure quizzes are:
- defensible
- reusable
- trustworthy
- aligned with classroom practice
